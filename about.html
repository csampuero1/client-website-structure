Our Story

Story of the Bakery

Founded with a desire to nourish our local community, North Star Bakery began in a small home kitchen. Today, we stand as a warm neighborhood fixture where commuters, families, and friends gather over the simple joy of freshly baked goods.

Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with dedication and passion. That is why our flour is sourced from regional organic mills.
