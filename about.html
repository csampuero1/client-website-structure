Our Story

Story of the Bakery

Founded with a desire to nourish our local community, North Star Bakery began in a small home kitchen. Today, we stand as a warm neighborhood fixture where commuters, families, and friends gather over the simple joy of freshly baked goods.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with great ingredients. That is why 100% of our flour is stone-ground and sourced from regional organic mills.

Staff Highlights

Our small team of dedicated bakers works through the night to ensure everything is fresh when our doors open.
